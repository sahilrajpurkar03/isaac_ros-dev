## Using your own robot or topics

# if you use your own robot or topics, change only the lines marked CHANGE in sim_nvblox.launch.py
#   1. cuVSLAM, the four remapping lines: the right-hand side of each line must be your own left/right image and camera_info topics (find the names with: ros2 topic list)
#   2. cuVSLAM, camera_optical_frames: the frame_id of your left and right camera images (find it with: ros2 topic echo <your image topic> --once --field header.frame_id)
#   3. cuVSLAM, base_frame and odom_frame: the names of your robot base frame and odometry frame
#   4. nvblox, the four remapping lines: your own depth image, depth camera_info, color image and color camera_info topics
# the lines marked TUNE depend on your robot: esdf_slice_height, esdf_slice_min_height and esdf_slice_max_height are the height band (in meters) that becomes the 2D grid, so obstacles inside this band are shown
# cuVSLAM publishes odom > base_link in this launch file, so switch your own odom > base_link publisher off (two publishers for the same frame give errors)
